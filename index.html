Research

Collective behavior, designed by evolution

Real robot teams are rarely identical. I ask how teams of different robots should be built and controlled, and let evolutionary algorithms search over neural controllers and team composition together.

All research

Evolving team composition

How many robots of each type does a task need? The mix and the behavior are designed as one.

Cooperative transport

Robots with different sensing ranges move an object none of them can move alone.

Collective exploration

How each robot's neural controller shapes what the whole swarm can discover.

Versatile controllers

Knowledge distillation so one compact controller handles several tasks.

Interactive

Can you beat evolution?

A robot team enters a building it has never seen. Scouts map it with LiDAR and find three heavy packages; workers push them to the dock in crews; everything travels over a radio mesh and every robot has a battery. With a fixed budget, design the team, click any robot to see its sensors, then let a genetic algorithm design its own team. It is the question behind my PhD, in a practical setting.
