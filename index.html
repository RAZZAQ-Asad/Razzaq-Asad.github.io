About me

I received my PhD from the University of Toyama in 2025, supported by a JST SPRING fellowship. During my doctorate, I visited IRIDIA at Université libre de Bruxelles. I now work at NISAR Lab, KUAS, on AI for human–robot interaction and multi-agent systems.
